## How to read this

- **Priority.**
  - **P1**: blocks trustworthy results or risks losing data.
  - **P2**: a correctness or honesty issue.
  - **P3**: cleanup.

  Rows are sorted by priority within each group.
- **Kind.**
  - **decision**: the owner's call. The cell gives its `O-xx` id in the [decision log](decisions/README.md), plus the [edge-rule memo](decisions/2026-09-22-biolean-transitive-edges.md) where it applies. 18 smaller decisions are marked "not yet in the log".
  - **task**: work anyone can do once the direction is clear. A task tied to a logged decision names that decision.
- **IDs** are stable; cite them in commits. The prefix is the group: R repo and housekeeping, M methods graph, G guardrail, S method-sequencing benchmark, K Biolean kernel, B Biolean benchmark and results.
- **Evidence** is a file:line at `6b28222`, a commit, or a command run from the repo root.
  - `mg` means `alias mg='.venv/bin/python -m methods_graph.cli'`.
  - Tags such as [DB], [GO], [net] and [paid] are defined in [README.md](README.md#running-the-cli).
  - Claims nobody re-checked on 2026-09-22 are marked **(unverified)**.
- **Watch for drift.** Every line number in the root `README.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md` and the specs and plans under `docs/superpowers/` refers to `6b28222`; read them with `git show 6b28222:<path>`. All of those files are being edited in the same 2026-09-22 docs pass. Items that are only about their wording (R9, R10, R11, R15, G15) may already be fixed in the working tree, and the new status banners on the specs and plans already record some of R13, S22, K15 and B9. Run `git diff` before acting on one.
